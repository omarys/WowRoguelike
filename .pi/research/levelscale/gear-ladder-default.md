# Gear by level — the five default classes (Warrior / Rogue / Priest / Druid / Mage)

Per-band, per-slot gear a real party is wearing at the top of each band, for
levels 23–27, 28–32, 33–37 and 38–40. Fills the 23–40 gap in
`warrior-rogue-ladder.md`, `mage-ladder.md` and `priest-druid-ladder.md`; same
table layout as `gear-ladder-rest.md` so `scripts/band-totals.py` can sum it.

`Conf` = `M` when both hosts agree on required level, armour, damage, speed and
all five stat lines; `U` when they disagree (see **Sources** — emberveil's stat
labels are unreliable, so the Wowhead Classic tooltip is the value used).
Weapon rows carry the item's own stats but `band-totals.py` sums armour slots
only, so the band totals below exclude the main-hand weapon's stat lines.

## Sources

Every row was read on both hosts on the same day; `Src` carries the pair of item ids
(`e` = database.emberveil.org, `w` = nether.wowhead.com/classic tooltip JSON, which is
the only Wowhead endpoint that answers plain fetches — `www.wowhead.com` returns 403).

- `https://database.emberveil.org/item/<id>` — required level, armour, damage range,
  speed and drop source. Cross-checks Wowhead on those five numbers for every row in
  this file without exception.
- `https://nether.wowhead.com/classic/tooltip/item/<id>?dataEnv=8&locale=0` — the five
  stat lines (authoritative: emberveil renders rating and spell-power slots under
  stat labels, e.g. *Batwing Mantle* shows `+3 Agility` where the item has
  "Increases spell power by 4", and *Mograine's Might* shows `+16 Spirit` for
  "+16 hit rating"). Rows where that mismatch trips are `U`; the Wowhead values are
  what the tables show.
- Reused and re-verified rows from `.pi/research/levelscale/gear-ladder-rest.md`
  (the Hunter / Shaman / Warlock leather, mail and cloth ladders) and
  `mage-ladder.md` / `priest-druid-ladder.md`, which had the 23–40 rows only in
  fragments and without the two-host check.
- Three rows inherited from those older files were wrong and are corrected here:
  *Ironshod Bludgeon* is `+20 Str / +8 Sta` (not `+8 / +20`), *Mograine's Might* has
  `+17 Sta` and no Spirit (the `+16` is melee hit rating), and *Illusionary Rod* has
  `+7 Sta / +15 Int` with `+10` spell crit (not `+10 Spirit`).
- `scripts/band-totals.py` header comment defines what the sim consumes: armour, the
  five stats, shield-ness, weapon damage/speed.

## Gaps

- **No unsourced slot.** All 12–14 slots in all 20 bands carry a real item; there is
  no `no sourced item` row anywhere in this file.
- **Trinket rows are deliberately absent.** Pre-40 vanilla trinkets carry no armour and
  none of the five stats (they are on-use/equip effects, e.g. *Nifty Stopwatch*), and
  `Content.fs` GearPiece has no trinket hook, so the slot would add nothing to a band
  total.
- **One weapon slot only.** Wands are real upgrades for Priest/Mage at every band
  (*Wand of Eventide* 27, *Necrotic Wand* 30, *Jaina's Firestarter* 37, *Umbral Wand*
  40) and are not modelled; the main-hand row is a staff for all three casters, so
  Priest and Mage share the same caster staff ladder (there is no separate pre-40
  caster weapon line in vanilla).
- **Rogue off-hand daggers** are listed for completeness but `band-totals.py` takes
  the last row whose slot contains `weapon` as *the* weapon, so the band weapon figure
  is main-hand only; the off-hand row contributes its stats to the totals.
- **Two shields that beat the listed one cannot be flagged as shields by the sim.**
  `band-totals.py` detects a shield from the *item name* containing "Shield", so
  *Thermaplugg's Central Core* (795 armour at 28, Gnomeregan) and *Aegis of the Scarlet
  Commander* (1548 armour at 39, Scarlet Commander Mograine in the Cathedral) are
  passed over for *Renegade Shield* and *Ravager's Shield*. Same slot, 100–170 less
  armour, but the shield flag survives.
- **The Warrior totals are shield-dominated**: at 27 the shield is 611 of 1852 armour,
  at 40 it is 1380 of 3673. Body armour alone runs 1241 → 2293 (1.85×), and the
  mail→plate step lands entirely in 38–40 (body 1452 in 33–37 → 2293). Compare Warrior
  to the other four classes on body armour, not on the headline number.
- **Era skew between hosts.** emberveil reports 1.12 required levels, Wowhead Classic
  reports the Classic Era value, which is sometimes lower (*Robes of Arugal* 24 vs 20,
  *Thermaplugg's Central Core* 32 vs 28). The tables use the Wowhead value; both are
  inside the band either way.
- **Rare world drops are used where the band has no dungeon alternative**, e.g.
  *Pendulum of Doom*-class BoE epics were rejected as twink gear, but
  *Caverndeep Trudgers*, *Petrolspill Leggings* and *Nordic Longshank* are ordinary
  BoE world drops with no dungeon source at that level.
