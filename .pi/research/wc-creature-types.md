## Practical CC consequences (direct from the type column)
- Polymorph / Sap legal: Druid of the Fang, Anacondra, Serpentis, Cobrahn, Pythas (all Humanoid). Sap additionally requires the target be out of combat.
- Scare Beast / Hibernate legal: Deviate Ravager only. Scare Beast illegal on every other creature here.
- Undead/Demon/Elemental/Dragonkin tools (Shackle, Turn Evil, Banish, Hibernate-on-dragonkin): no legal target among these 8.

## Evidence notes
- Direct: all type values, Cobrahn's snake shift, Pythas' adds + "druid polymorphed" wording, Mutanus' Shadow immunity, Mutanus' three abilities (thewowdb excerpt via search).
- Interpretation: mapping "immune to Shadow spells" onto Fear; reading empty wiki "Abilities" sections as "nothing documented" rather than "no abilities".
- Unresolved: no per-boss immunity data on the wiki pages (their Classic ability tables extracted empty). No authoritative statement found that the Fanglords *are* polymorphable in Classic, only that the wiki's Pythas strategy assumes the druid add can be polymorphed.
