# Level 24 Warrior Tank — Real WoW Classic (1.12 / Classic Era 1.15) Numbers

Scope note up front: **there is no canonical level-24 twink bracket in Classic Era** (brackets are 10–19, 20–29, 30–39…). All published twink BiS lists are for 19/29/39/49. The set below is therefore a *constructed* "best item you can actually equip at 24" list: every item is individually sourced and has required level ≤ 24 (or is quest-gated below that), but the set as a whole is researcher-selected, not copied from a bracket guide. Treat item-by-item selections as **researcher inference** on top of sourced item stats.

Version note: values below are vanilla/Classic-Era values. `foreverdb.net` pages explicitly compare against "Classic Era" and state the numbers are unchanged for the abilities cited.

## 5. Health pool and resource behaviour

**Health.** Vanilla rule: ~10 health per point of Stamina (documented; the classic low-level tables are consistent with it for stamina ≥ 20). Base class health at level 24 = 253 per the game-data table.

- Health from stamina: 91 × 10 = **910**
- Base health, level 24: **253**
- **Estimated total ≈ 1,163 HP** (≈1,160)
- **Confidence: medium.** The 10 HP/Stamina rule is solid; whether the 253 base term is additive at level 24 is my reading of the game-data table (**researcher inference**). Level-1 cross-check behaves oddly (Human warrior level 1 = 60 HP with 22 Stamina per classic-wow-archive/vanilla-wow-archive), i.e. low-level health is not a clean 10×Stamina, so the exact low-level additive constant is uncertain. Sources: https://docs.rs/wow_world_base/.../base_stats.rs.html, https://classic-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Warrior

**Rage.** Rage conversion constant at level 24: c = 0.0091107836×24² + 3.225598133×24 + 4.2652911 = **86.93** (https://zugzugdb.com/formulas/rage, same constants as the vanilla 1.12 emu formula on forum.nostalrius.org).

- Rage from damage **dealt**: 7.5 × d / 86.93 → **1 rage per ~11.6 damage**
- Rage from damage **taken**: 2.5 × d / 86.93 → **1 rage per ~34.8 damage taken**
- Weapon-speed term: R = (7.5·d/c + f)/2, with f = speed×3.5 on a hit, speed×7 on a crit → at 2.70 speed, f = 9.45 (hit) / 18.9 (crit)
- Auto-attack of ~85 damage lands ≈ **8.4 rage per swing** (~3.1 rage/sec) (**inference** from the sourced formula)
- Charge adds **9 rage** instantly (rank 1)
- Max rage 100. No other resource at 24 (no mana use; warrior mana = 0, Spell Details on Classic-era DB pages still show a vestigial "150 mana"/"200 mana" cost field which is legacy data, not a real cost).

## 8. +Attack power / +crit from gear and enchants (twink route)

**From the gear set itself:** **+0 attack power, +0% crit.** Every verified piece is Str/Agi/Sta/Spirit only. (Str still converts to AP at 2 AP/Str, already counted in the 226 AP.)

**Verified enchants usable at this level:**
- **Enchant Weapon – Fiery Weapon**: "often strike for **40** additional fire damage" — https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Formula:_Enchant_Weapon_-_Fiery_Weapon , https://wowhead.com/classic/spell=13898/enchant-weapon-fiery-weapon. Proc is per-minute (PPM) based, so a **slower** weapon procs more per swing. **+40 fire on proc, +0 AP, +0 crit.**
- **Enchant Bracer – Superior Stamina**: **+9 Stamina** → +90 HP. https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Superior_Stamina_(bracer_enchant)

**Commonly used twink enchants I did NOT verify this pass (treat as UNVERIFIED):** Enchant Weapon – Agility (+15 Agi), Enchant Weapon – Crusader (+100 Str proc), Enchant Chest – Major Health (+100 HP), Enchant Chest – Greater Stats (+4 all), Enchant Gloves – Superior Agility (+15 Agi), Enchant Boots – Greater Agility (+7 Agi). If applied, the realistic ceiling is roughly **+15 Agi from weapon, +15 Agi gloves, +7 Agi boots, +9 Sta bracer, +100 HP chest** → about **+37 Agi / +9 Sta / +100 HP** (≈ +1.8% crit territory at 60 values, but **level-24 crit conversion UNVERIFIED**), and the weapon enchant can only be one of Fiery/Crusader/Agility.

## Contradictions recorded

1. **River Pride Choker** (neck candidate): warcraft.wiki.gg says item level 19, **+1 Str / +2 Sta, requires level 12** (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/River_Pride_Choker); wowhead Classic says item level 33, **+4 Str / +9 Sta, requires level 28** (https://wowhead.com/classic/item=13087/river-pride-choker). Unresolvable here → excluded from the set.
2. **Sparkleshell Mantle stats**: wowclassicdatabase/classicdb/foreverdb say **161 armor, +10 Sta, +4 Agi @ req 24**; wowdb.net shows **15 armor, +9 Sta** (https://wowdb.net/item-13131.html). I used the three-site majority (161 armor) and flag the outlier.
3. **Fiery Weapon damage**: formula page and spell page say **40** fire (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Formula:_Enchant_Weapon_-_Fiery_Weapon); the `Fiery_Weapon` enchant page text says **34** ("cannot be applied to items higher than level 136") — that text is from a later/other variant (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fiery_Weapon). I report 40 for Classic.
4. **Taunt cooldown**: Classic Era = **10 s** per foreverdb's "Classic Era" column; a beta/other client variant shows 8 s. Reported: 10 s.
5. **Beetle Clasps armor**: 83 (wowhead/wowclassicdatabase) vs 82 (warcraft.wiki.gg). Used 83.
6. **Sunder Armor threat**: whether threat scales with rank is debated in community threads (https://reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/d52oxl/) — tooltip says only "high amount of threat". No number reported.
