# Level 24 Rogue (Vanilla 1.12 / Classic Era 1.15) — sourced numbers

Web tools were available (web_search + fetch_content + source_check all ran). Everything below is either
**VERIFIED** (a fetched source states it) or **UNVERIFIED / INFERENCE** (explicitly labelled).
Primary data sources used: classicdb.ch (1.12 item/spell database), the `wow_world_base` vanilla base-stat
tables (autogenerated from 1.12 data), mangos `player_levelstats`, warcraft.wiki.gg, wow-guides.com
(1.12-derived), xpoff.com (measured level-19 twink data), Warcraft Tavern 29-twink guide.

Race chosen: **Night Elf** (Alliance). Reason: highest base Agility of the seven rogue races at 24
(NE 60 vs Human 55 vs Orc 52), and Alliance unlocks Tunic of Westfall. Racial Quickness is dodge, not crit.

estimate, **not** a sourced number — do not treat it as data.

## 5. Health and energy pools

- **Base health 301** at level 24 (mangos `player_levelstats` basehp, class 4, level 24 = 301; matches the
  `wow_world_base` hp column).
- **Health = 301 + 10 × Stamina** (Stamina = 10 health per point, first points aside — wow-guides states the
  tenfold rate and notes the low-level quirk: "The first 20 points give 1 health each, so the tenfold rate
  starts above that", https://wow-guides.com/knowledge/attributes).
  - Verified-gear set: 301 + 10×50 = **801 health**.
  - Estimated full set (§3C): **≈ 1050–1200 health** (estimate).
- **Energy pool = 100 maximum**, regenerating **20 energy per 2-second tick (10/sec)**; the only way to raise
  the cap is the 31-point Assassination talent Vigor (+10, unavailable at 24).
  - https://www.wowhead.com/classic/resource/energy-3 ("maximum of 100 … every two seconds character
    regenerate 20 energy points, by tick")
  - https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Energy (energy "capped at 100" under most circumstances)
  - Rogue trainer data confirms **Dual Wield is learned at level 10** (so a 24 rogue dual-wields):
    https://wowclassicdb.com/npc/1411 ("Dual Wield | 10")

## 7. Ability tooltips at level 24 (exact ranks, exact numbers)

All from classicdb.ch (1.12 spell data). Class 4 = rogue.

### Backstab — **Rank 3** is the highest usable at 24 (learned at level **20**)
- Tooltip (verbatim from classicdb, spell 2590): *"Backstab the target, causing 150% weapon damage plus 48 to
  the target. Must be behind the target. Requires a dagger in the main hand. Awards 1 combo point."*
- Cost **60 energy**, melee range, instant, no cooldown; awards **1 combo point**.
- Spell effects: `(121) Normalized Weapon Damage + 32`, `(31) Weapon % Dmg 150`, `(80) Add Combo Points`.
- **Formula (1.12): damage = ((weapon damage + AP/14 × 1.7) × 1.5) + 48**, the 1.7 being the dagger
  normalization coefficient — https://classicroguecraft.com/formulas-spreadsheets/
  (`backstab_dmg = ((weapon_dmg + AP/14 * 1.7) * 1.5 + 210) * (1 + 0.04 * opportunity_rank)`, i.e. the same
  shape with the rank constant in place of 210); normalization background:
  https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Normalization
- **Important correction to the brief:** vanilla Backstab does **not** add a second bonus for being behind —
  being behind the target is a *requirement* for the 150% to happen at all. (The modern "damage increased by
  20% when you are behind your target" wording is the retail tooltip: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Backstab,
  retail version.)
- With Assassin's Blade + 146 AP: min ≈ (20 + 17.7)×1.5 + 48 = **105**, max ≈ (39 + 17.7)×1.5 + 48 = **133**
  (computed, not a tooltip).
- **Not available at 24:** Backstab **Rank 4** (level **28**, "150% weapon damage plus 69",
  https://classicdb.ch/?spell=2591).

### Sinister Strike — **Rank 4** (level **22**)
- *"An instant strike that causes 15 damage in addition to your normal weapon damage. Awards 1 combo point."*
- **45 energy**, effect `(121) Normalized Weapon Damage + 15`.
- Source: https://classicdb.ch/?spell=1759 (Rank 4, level 22). Rank 1 (level 1) = +3 damage,
  https://classicdb.ch/?spell=1752.
- **Not available at 24:** the next rank; trainer data lists Sinister Strike ranks taught at levels
  6 / 14 / 22 / **30** — https://wowclassicdb.com/npc/1411.

### Eviscerate — **Rank 4** (level **24** — exactly at the cap)
- *"Finishing move that causes damage per combo point, increased by Attack Power:"*
  **1 cp 41–61 | 2 cp 72–92 | 3 cp 103–123 | 4 cp 134–154 | 5 cp 165–185**
- **35 energy**, school Physical. Source: https://classicdb.ch/?spell=6762 (Rank 4, level 24).
- **Not available at 24:** Eviscerate Rank 5 (level 32) and above.

### Kick — **Rank 1** (level **12**)
- *"A quick kick that injures a single foe for 15 damage. It also interrupts spellcasting and prevents any
  spell in that school from being cast for 5 sec."*
- **25 energy**. Source: https://classicdb.ch/?spell=1766 (Rank 1, level 12).
- **Not available at 24:** **Rank 2 = level 26**, 30 damage (https://classicdb.ch/?spell=1767) — so a 24 rogue
  has only the 15-damage Kick.

### Gouge — **Rank 2** (level **18**)
- *"Causes 20 damage, incapacitating the opponent for 4 sec, and turns off your attack. Target must be facing
  you. Any damage caused will revive the target. Awards 1 combo point."*
- **45 energy**, 4 s incapacitate. Source: https://classicdb.ch/?spell=1777 (Rank 2, level 18).
- **Not available at 24:** **Rank 3 = level 32**, 32 damage (https://classicdb.ch/?spell=8629).

### Cheap Shot — **NOT AVAILABLE AT 24**

  stealthed. Awards 2 combo points."*, 60 energy — https://classicdb.ch/?spell=1833

- Also absent at 24: Kidney Shot (level 30, per trainer list https://wowclassicdb.com/npc/1411).

## 8. Attack power, and agility → crit at level 24

**Attack power from gear.**
- Rogue conversion: **1 AP per Strength and 1 AP per Agility** (rogues/hunters get 1 melee AP per Agi; classes
  other than war/pal/sham/druid get 1 AP per Str) — https://wow-guides.com/knowledge/attributes ("Strength:
  2 attack power, or 1 per point … Warriors, paladins, shamans and druids get 2 attack power per point;
  everyone else gets 1"; "Agility … Rogues and hunters also get attack power from it — 1 melee for both").
- **Gear AP contribution** (verified set): Agi +40 and Str +10 → **+50 attack power from gear**.
- **Total AP at 24 with the verified set: 146** (Str 46 + Agi 100). Base-only AP (naked) = **96**.
  (146 AP = +10.4 DPS on a 1.90-speed dagger, or +17.7 per Backstab/Sinister Strike via the 1.7 dagger
  normalization — computed from the AP formula
  `Weapon Damage increase from Attack Power = (Weapon Speed × Attack Power)/14`,
  https://xpoff.com/threads/in-depth-stat-guide-for-19s-with-scaling-formulas.93237/ .)

**Crit per agility — the real ratio.**
- **Level 60 rogue: 28.99 Agility per 1% crit** (0.0345%/Agi) — 1.12 data,
  https://wow-guides.com/knowledge/attributes ; the round "29 Agi = 1% crit" figure quoted everywhere is the
  level-60 number only (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Critical_strike table lists Rogue 29 at L60).
- **Level 19 rogue (measured): 0.1316% melee crit per Agility = 7.60 Agility per 1% crit**, "confirmed by
  Symandria", in https://xpoff.com/threads/in-depth-stat-guide-for-19s-with-scaling-formulas.93237/
- **Level 1 rogue: 2.3 Agility per 1% crit** — https://wow-guides.com/knowledge/attributes
- **Level 24: no direct source found → UNVERIFIED.** The ratio rises with level (Blizzard's own forum thread
  documents that it changes every level and that no published formula exists:
  https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/formula-for-calculating-agility-needed-per-crit/534418).
  Linear interpolation between the measured level-19 (7.60) and level-60 (28.99) points gives
  **≈ 10.2 Agility per 1% crit (≈ 0.098% crit per Agi) at level 24** — this is a researcher INFERENCE, not a
  sourced number.
- Consequence at 24 with the verified set: 100 Agi ÷ ~10.2 ≈ **9.8% melee crit** (inference), all of it from
  agility; **no item in the verified set adds flat crit %**, and a 24 rogue has no Malice points unless
  invested (5% per point is the talent, not gear — https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/quick-rogue-stat-question/756575
  for the general "29 agi = 1% at 60" discussion).

## Contradictions / disputes recorded

1. **The brief's Backstab premise is retail, not vanilla.** Vanilla Backstab rank 3 is "150% weapon damage
   plus 48", and being behind is mandatory, not an extra bonus. Retail's tooltip adds a separate behind bonus
   and a %-of-AP coefficient (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Backstab retail version vs
   https://classicdb.ch/?spell=2590 dual-wield: also
   https://www.forevervsclassic.com/spells/rogue/backstab). Use the vanilla numbers above.
2. **"29 Agility = 1% crit" is level-60 only.** Level 19 measured is 7.6 Agi/%; level 1 is 2.3. Applying 29 at
   level 24 understates crit by ~3×.
3. **Base-attribute tables disagree by edition.** warcraft.wiki.gg's `Base attributes` page is the *retail*
   table (columns for DK/Monk, rogue Agi 62 at 24) and does **not** reproduce vanilla values; it was rejected
   in favour of the 1.12 table + mangos cross-check.
4. **Tunic of Westfall shows no level requirement** on classicdb/wowhead ("Requires Level" line absent, item
   level 24) — it is a quest reward from an ~level 18 Alliance chain, so it is usable at 24, but if your
   balance file needs an explicit req, record it as "quest-gated, no numeric req".
5. **Orc rogue base stats at levels 19–20 are duplicated** in the vanilla `BaseStats` table (no growth); do not
   use Orc 19/20 rows. Orc level 24 is consistent.

## Missing evidence (explicit gaps)

- **Head, hands, legs, feet, ring 2, trinket**: no item with level req ≤ 24 could be verified as BiS for those
  slots. At 24 those slots are filled with random-suffix greens (item level 28–29 → req 23/24, "of the Monkey"
  = Agi/Sta, "of the Tiger" = Agi/Str) whose exact suffix values vary per item; I did not verify a single
  concrete roll.
- **Agility-per-1%-crit at level 24** — interpolated only (see §8).
- **Off-hand 50% damage / dual-wield miss penalty** — not verified from a fetched source.
- **WSG reputation requirement** for Sentinel's Medallion / Protector's Band (classicdb lists only req 18 and a
  2g price; the Warcraft Tavern 29 guide says Honored) — unresolved.
- **source_check validation limitation:** `source_check` on the Backstab rank-3 claim returned
  "missing-evidence (confidence 0.20), no passages retrieved" (it surfaced candidate URLs but no excerpts).
  The claim therefore rests on the fetched classicdb spell page (spell 2590) and the wiki's Classic tooltip,
  not on an automated passage check.
- **Enchants** (e.g. +15 Agi gloves, +3 Agi cloak, +4 stats chest) are excluded entirely — item-only numbers
  as requested.
