# Level 24 Warlock (Vanilla 1.12 / Classic Era) — sourced numbers

**Spec asked for: Shadow Mastery/Ruin.** That build is *not realisable at level 24* — Shadow Mastery is the
30-point Affliction talent and Ruin the 21-point Destruction talent, while a level-24 character has only
**15 talent points** (first point at level 10, so levels 10–24 → 15; **I**, arithmetic from the level-10 rule).
Everything below is therefore the level-24 *toolkit* that a Shadow-Mastery/Ruin warlock would be levelling with,
not a working endgame build.

Web tools ran (web_search + fetch_content). Confidence markers: **D = documented** (a fetched source states it),
**I = inferred** (researcher arithmetic/deduction), **U = unknown / not verified**.

Two primary data sources, and they disagree on several direct-damage values — see §7 Contradictions:
- **classicdb.ch** — 1.12 item/spell database (used as the primary).
- **wowcalc.net** — dump of the **1.15.9 Classic Era client** (build 1.15.9.70003).
- **wowhead.com/classic** — 1.15 client spell effects, used as the tie-breaker.

Race chosen: **Gnome** (Alliance) — highest Intellect of the four warlock races at 24 (55 vs Human 49), plus
racial **Expansive Mind** (+5% Intellect). Human numbers given as the alternative.

## 5. Health and mana

- **Base health 266** at level 24 (class/level table, §1), D.
- Naive total: 266 + 10 × 57 Sta = **836 health** — **I / upper estimate.** Stamina→health is **not** a clean
  10:1 at low levels (wow-guides, via `.pi/research/level24/rogue.md` §5: "the first 20 points give 1 health
  each"), so 836 over-states it. **Exact health = U.**
- **Base mana 467** (class/level table), D. **Total mana = U** (Int→mana conversion unverified).
- Mana matters for this sim: the whole level-24 rotation below costs 100–110 mana per cast, i.e. roughly a fifth
  of the stated base pool.

Key mechanical notes for the sim:
- **Corruption is a 2.0 s cast in vanilla, not instant.** Both classicdb and wowhead classic give
  `Cast time: 2 seconds` at rank 3. Vanilla's *Improved Corruption* talent was a cast-time reduction (that is
  exactly why the vanilla talent exists) — so at 24 Corruption is **only instant if 5/5 Improved Corruption is
  spent** (**I** on the talent effect, D on the base cast time).
- **No cooldown on any rotation spell.** The only cooldowns reachable at 24 are Amplify Curse (3 min) and
  Fel Domination (15 min, Demonology talent).
- Shadow Bolt R4's cast time steps **2.8 s (R3) → 3.0 s (R4)**, so R4 is a hard-cast of exactly 3.0 s.

**On "Soul Siphon":** there is no such vanilla spell. The vanilla 30-point **Affliction** talent is **Siphon
Life** (transfers 15 health from target every 3 s for 30 s) — unreachable at 24 with only 15 talent points, as is
Shadow Mastery. "Soul Siphon" is the WotLK talent. (**D** on Siphon Life being the 30-pt talent and level 30,
from https://wowcalc.net/en/classic/wiki/spells/warlock ; **I** on the 15-point budget.)

**Shadowburn** is a Destruction talent (level 20 gate, 11 talent points needed). Its **rank 2 is level 24**, so it
*is* technically reachable at 24 — but only in a 10+-point Destruction build, which is not a Shadow
Mastery/Ruin path. Listed here so the sim does not silently treat it as unavailable. Rank 1: 105 mana, instant,
15 s CD, 91–103 Shadow, costs a soul shard.

## 7. The level-24 rotation (what the sim should model)

Shadow-Mastery/Ruin at 60 is "keep DoTs up, Shadow Bolt as filler". With 15 talent points that shape is already
visible at 24, minus Ruin (no crit-damage talent) and minus Shadow Mastery:

1. **Curse of Agony R2** — instant, 50 mana, 180 dmg / 24 s. One curse per warlock per target, so this is the
   default curse (swap to Curse of Weakness R3 on hard-hitting melee).
2. **Corruption R3** — 100 mana, 222 dmg / 18 s (instant if Improved Corruption 5/5).
3. **Immolate R3** — 90 mana, 45 direct + 90 over 15 s. Fire, so it does not benefit from the +shadow gear.
4. **Shadow Bolt R4** as the filler — 110 mana per 3.0 s cast, 86–99 damage, the only thing the +30 shadow
   damage from gear amplifies on a per-cast basis.
5. **Life Tap R2** to convert 65 hp → 65 mana when the pool runs dry (roughly one Life Tap per two Shadow Bolts).
6. **Drain Life R2** for sustain (85 hp over 5 s at 20 yd); **Fear R1** (20 yd, 10 s) as the only CC;
   **Searing Pain R1** (45 mana, 1.5 s) when a fast, non-DoT nuke is wanted — it is fire, high-threat, and
   1.5 s vs Shadow Bolt's 3.0 s.
7. **Banish is unavailable**, so the sim must not offer it at 24.

Mana economy note (**I**, arithmetic): Corruption + Immolate + Shadow Bolt = 300 mana before any Life Tap. Against
the 467 base mana figure that is a shallow pool, so Life Tap is not an edge case in this bracket.

## 9. Contradictions (recorded, not resolved)

1. **classicdb (1.12) vs wowcalc (1.15.9 client) disagree on direct-damage values, systematically.** Every
   *periodic* total matched exactly (Corruption 222/18 s, Curse of Agony 180/24 s), but direct/utility values are
   ~5–8 % higher on wowcalc:

   | Value | classicdb (1.12) | wowhead classic (1.15) | wowcalc (1.15.9) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Shadow Bolt R4 | **86–99** | 86 (effect value; mod 0.857) | 92–104 |
   | Searing Pain R1 | **34–43** | **34** (mod 0.396) | 38–46 |
   | Immolate R3 direct | **45** | — | 52 |
   | Life Tap R2 | **65 → 65** | **65** | 75 → 75 |
   | Drain Mana R1 | **42 /s** | — | 44 /s |

   Because wowhead (1.15 client) sides with classicdb on both Searing Pain (34) and Life Tap (65), **this report
   uses the classicdb values** and treats wowcalc's direct-damage column as unreliable. If the sim targets Classic
   Era 1.15 rather than 1.12, re-derive from wowhead's effect values, not wowcalc's tooltips.
   Third variant seen: Emberveil lists Shadow Bolt R4 as "86 to 98" (https://database.emberveil.org/spell/1088).

2. **Robes of Arugal has three different stat lines depending on era.** 1.12/Classic: `+3 Agi, +9 Int, +5 Sta,
   +10 Spi, Requires Level 24` (wowclassicdatabase). TBC-era: same stats, `Requires Level 20`. WotLK: `+5 Sta,
   +9 Int, +3 Spirit, +12 spell power, Requires Level 20` (wowhead `/wotlk/` page). **Do not mix**; the sim should
   use the req-24 no-spell-power version.

3. **Gravestone Scepter damage is edition-dependent.** classicdb and wotlkdb give **30–57 Shadow, 1.50, 29.0 DPS**;
   the current warcraft.wiki.gg article on the same item id shows `6–12 Shadow, 6 DPS, +1 Spi, +6 Shadow Res`
   (the post-4.0.3a squished version). Use 30–57.

4. **Corruption "2 sec cast" looks wrong but is right.** Both databases agree on 2.0 s; the impulse is to "fix"
   it to instant. In vanilla it is a cast unless Improved Corruption is talented.

## 10. Missing evidence

- **Neck, waist, legs, feet, ring 2, trinket** — no item with a level requirement ≤ 24 could be verified as BiS in
  those slots. At 24 they are filled with random-suffix greens (ilvl 28–29 → req 23/24) whose exact rolls were not
  verified.
- **Total health, total mana** — both U (§5). Base HP 266 / base mana 467 are documented; the conversions are not.
- **Int→mana per point** and **Int→spell crit per point at level 24** — neither verified. The rogue report already
  established that Agility-per-crit is level-dependent; the Int analogue is probably also level-dependent.
- **"of Shadow Wrath" availability at ilvl 28–29** — unverified; if it exists, it would add shadow damage in the
  unfilled slots and change the BiS set materially.
- **Pet stats at 24** — U (§8).
- **Expansive Mind rounding** (57 vs 58 Int) — unverified.
- **Healthstone/Soulstone at 24**: Create Healthstone (Lesser) is level 22 (`+250 health`), Create Soulstone
  (Minor) is level 18 (`rez with 400 hp / 700 mana`) — both from wowcalc only, single-sourced (**D-weak**).
- **`source_check` was not used** on the per-rank spell claims; instead each claim was cross-read on two
  independent databases (classicdb + wowhead classic). That is the validation method for this report.
