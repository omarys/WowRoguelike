## 2. Class mechanics

**The elemental shield becomes a charged, timed, exclusive slot.** Lightning Shield "runs on a duration
with globes"; both new shields carry globes; "Only one elemental shield can be active at a time." Three
spells the sim does not carry at all (its note: "Lightning Shield and Rockbiter are left out — nothing
in the vocabulary retaliates or imbues a weapon").

**Threat becomes per-source.** Spirit Armor ("the threat you generate while wearing one"), Totemic
Alignment ("Transfers part of the threat your totems generate onto you, so they stop being killed"),
Calming Winds ("Reduces threat from your physical attacks, weapon imbues, Lightning Strike and
Stormstrike, **but not while Rockbiter Weapon is active**"), Nature's Grace ("Reduces the threat
generated by your Nature spells"). Threat is per *school* and per *imbue state*, not one party-wide
multiplier.

**Imbues become queryable state.** Elemental Weapons is "Completely rebuilt. Each imbue grants its own
benefit: Flametongue builds Fire damage as you hit, Frostbrand and the others each do something
distinct" — and Calming Winds' Rockbiter clause proves a second talent branches on which imbue is up.

**Totems stop being scenery.** Totemic Mastery "Increases the duration of your helpful totems", Totemic
Recall refunds their mana, Totemic Alignment gives them threat, Improved Fire Totems raises Searing
Totem's "attack speed and range".

## 5. What this costs in `src/WowRoguelike.Core/Content.fs`

The sim already models this Shaman as **0/5/46** (`let private shamanTalents` and the "Shaman 0/5/46"
comment above it). Axes: `HealDoneBp · HealCostBp · IntellectBp · RegenBp · ManaRefundOnCritBp ·
CritArmorBp · HealingWayBp · SpiritToHealingBp · TotemEffectBp · Spells[CostBp,HealBp,CritBp,
CastTicksOff] · CooldownOff`, a closed `Effect` union, `AbilityDef.kitAt`. No number below is invented.

**(a) Expressible today — 7 of the 16 talents the build takes.**
- **Tidal Mastery 5/5** (6:5) — row move only; `CritBp` already carries it.
- **Tidal Focus 5/5 at 3/5** (5:3) — `HealCostBp`/`Spells.CostBp`; the new "also reduces the mana cost of
  your totems" retargets the existing `totemDiscount` (five `CostBp = 2500` rows) onto it.
- **Healing Focus 5 → 2** (9:2) — rank count only; pushback lives outside `Talents`.
- **Healing Way 3/3** (11:3) — `HealingWayBp = 1800` unchanged; only the applier list widens.
- **Restorative Totems 5/5** (13:5 or 14:3) — `TotemEffectBp = 2500` unchanged + a `CostBp` line on "Mana
  Spring Totem".
- **Improved Chain Heal 5/5** (21:5) — one `CastTicksOff` line, but Chain Heal is not a kit row
  (`kitAt [shamanLightningBoltRanks; earthShockRanks; flameShockRanks; shamanHealingWaveRanks]`), so the
  row must exist before the talent has anything to modify.
- **Improved Healing Wave 5/5** (1:5 or 2:5, identity unverified) — already `CastTicksOff`.

**(b) Needs a new model axis — 7 of 16.** Axis named per talent:
- **Ancestral Knowledge 5/5** (tree1 1:5) — "the total value of **all** your stats"; sim has only
  `IntellectBp`. Axis: **stat-wide multiplier**.
- **Spirit Link 1/1** (25:1) — axis: **damage redistribution between entities**; no `Effect` lets one
  actor's damage be split across others.
- **Improved Water Shield 3/3** (14:3) — axis: **shield charges as readable state** ("for each globe
  still active").
- **Undertow 2/2** (16:2) and **Tidal Surge 2/2** (19:2) — axis: **cast-triggered proc**; `Effect` has no
  on-cast trigger.
- **Ancestral Swiftness 1/1** (18:1) — axis: **effectiveness modifier on an empowered cast**; `Empowers`
  cannot hold "damaging spells cast this way have reduced effectiveness".
- **Nature's Grace 3/3** (8:3) — axis: **per-school threat multiplier**.

**(c) Collapses or renames kit rows — 2 of 16.**
- **Ancestral Swiftness** (18:1) renames the `naturesSwiftness` row; the talent that granted it is gone.
- **Spirit Link** (25:1) deletes `totem "Mana Tide Totem" 240 (secTicks 300.0) …` — "replaces Mana Tide
  Totem" — and takes the capstone.

Three more collapse though **not** in the build, each deleting something the sim pays for: **Purification**
("replaces Purification") strands `HealDoneBp = 1000`; **Totemic Focus** strands `totemDiscount`;
**Nature's Swiftness** and **Mana Tide Totem** strand kit rows.

## 6. The build decoded

`-F-FoDoaLFYQKFAB` = **0 / 5 / 46**: tree0 `{}`, tree1 `{1:5}`, tree2
`{1:5, 2:5, 5:3, 6:5, 8:3, 9:2, 10:1, 11:3, 13:5, 14:3, 16:2, 18:1, 19:2, 21:5, 25:1}` = 46 (51/51).

Tree identity rests on two facts, not index order: the page lists its trees Elemental → Enhancement →
Restoration, and `Content.fs` already models this exact 0/5/46 Shaman with Ancestral Knowledge 5/5 as
the five-point dip. So **tree0 Elemental (0, abandoned), tree1 Enhancement (5), tree2 Restoration (46)**.

**It commits to Restoration; the 31-point talent is Spirit Link** (slot 25, 1 rank, Row 7) — "Row 7, 1
Rank: new capstone, replaces Mana Tide Totem". Slot 25 is the only Row-7 slot it spends, and the sim's
Shaman is a healer, which is what Restoration buys. A 4-per-row layout (7 × 4 = the 28 slots the codec
assumes) makes 0-3 / 4-7 / 8-11 / 12-15 / 16-19 / 20-23 / 24-27 the rows, and under it Row 7 holds
exactly one 1-rank talent.

Slot→talent by confidence. **Corroborated** (page row *and* rank count match, nothing else listed fits):
`18:1` Ancestral Swiftness (only 1-rank Row 5), `21:5` Improved Chain Heal (Row 6, 5 Ranks), `25:1`
Spirit Link, `9:2` Healing Focus (only "5 → 2 Ranks" in Row 3), `14:3` Improved Water Shield (Row 4, 3
Ranks), `16:2`/`19:2` Undertow + Tidal Surge, `5:3`/`6:5` Tidal Focus (3/5) + Tidal Mastery (Row 4 →
Row 2), `8:3`/`11:3` Nature's Grace + Healing Way. **UNVERIFIED identity:** `1:5`, `2:5`, `10:1`, `13:5`.

Taken vs left: it takes **Improved Chain Heal**, but the sim's Shaman has no Chain Heal, so that
talent's whole effect is unmodelled; it takes no Bloodlust, Earthquake, Stormstrike, Lightning Strike,
Elemental Weapons, Spirit Armor, Ancestral Guardian, Calming Winds or Totemic Alignment; it pays 5 for
Ancestral Knowledge, whose "all your stats" the sim narrows to Intellect; and it drops the two talents
the model is *built* on — Purification's `HealDoneBp` and Totemic Focus's `totemDiscount` — because
OctoWoW deleted both.

## 7. Missing evidence / UNVERIFIED

- **The talent records were never retrieved.** The raw payload is ~30 KB and the shell replaces any tool
  result above a few KB with a fragment; `curl`/`python`/reader proxies all hit Cloudflare ("Just a
  moment please…"), the Wayback snapshot of `/talents/shaman/` is itself a Cloudflare interstitial, and
  context-mode's indexer is dead (`v1.0.169 → v2.0.0`). **Every slot→name mapping in §6 is inference
  from row + rank agreement, not a payload read.**
- **No coefficients on the live page.** "A percentage of weapon damage", "a share of it", "part of the
  mana" are not implementable. **UNVERIFIED.**
- **Earthquake contradicts itself:** "Row 7, **1 Rank**: new capstone (**three ranks at 40 / 50 / 60**)".
  Talent and spell ranks conflated; the build treats it as 1.
- **The revisions disagree and the live one won.** ARCH's Stormstrike (2 ranks, 12 sec, 12% base mana)
  has no live counterpart; ARCH gives all three race spells 21% base mana and no race labels, against
  the live Orc / Tauren / Troll with levels 40 / — / 30. Which revision OctoWoW runs is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **The brief for this note is wrong about the totems.** It says four — "Searing, Stoneclaw, Healing
  Stream, Mana Spring" — that "never expire". `totemKit` builds **five: Stoneskin, Grace of Air, Mana
  Tide, Healing Stream, Mana Spring**, with `Lifetime = None` as a deliberate, commented rule. No
  Searing, no Stoneclaw row.
- **Whether the calculator is OctoWoW or vanilla-plus-partial** is inferred. For: Elemental Mastery at
  Row 5, Reverberation at 3 ranks, a Row-7 Earthquake — none exist in 1.12.
